# AI Dev Story 标志

当前使用：ai-dev-story-logo-v2.png。2026-10-19 使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，透明背景，作为本项目标题标志。未使用 CLI 或外部 API 密钥。ai-dev-story-logo.png 保留初稿；v2 按用户反馈将 AI 改为青蓝色、DEV 保持橙黄色，并增大间距。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create an original, production-ready title logo for a playful satirical pixel-art AI-company management game. Text must read exactly "AI DEV STORY", spelled A I / D E V / S T O R Y, no other words. Two lines: AI DEV above STORY. Cheerful Japanese management-game title treatment: very bold rounded block letters with subtle stepped pixel corners, dark forest-charcoal outlines, warm cream/white outer keyline, clearly separated shallow dimensional shadows and crisp highlights. Top lettering golden yellow into warm orange; bottom lettering lively leaf green and mint. Surround the wordmark with a small original pixel microchip and an ascending score-chart motif in teal, plus at most two small pixel sparkles. Match a warm white, sage green and gold office-game UI. Strong silhouette, highly readable at 300px width, clean deliberate shapes, polished game identity. Original custom letterforms and composition, not a traced or copied existing game logo, no city skyline, controller, existing brand emblem or character. The wordmark is the hero, tightly framed with a modest transparent margin. Wide compact horizontal composition about 2:1. Truly transparent background, isolated complete logo, no backdrop, no mockup, no watermark.

## 清晰区分 AI 与 DEV 的编辑提示词

Edit this original AI DEV STORY game logo. Keep its wording exactly "AI DEV STORY", chunky pixel-styled typography, dark outlines, cream outer keyline, shallow dimensional shadows, chip and ascending-chart motifs, lower green STORY lettering, overall cheerful management-game identity and transparent background. Make only this legibility revision: change the top word "AI" to a vivid cyan-to-blue/teal palette, keep "DEV" golden-yellow-to-orange, and increase the visible gap between "AI" and "DEV" to roughly the width of the letter I. The two top words must read as distinct units even when the entire logo is displayed 300 pixels wide. Preserve the scale and balance of all text; no new wording or elements. Truly transparent background, complete logo uncropped.
